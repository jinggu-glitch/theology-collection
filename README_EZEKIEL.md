## Counting rules (read this before any number)

1. **Text:** Westminster Leningrad Codex (WLC), consonantal text. Vowels, accents, spaces, maqaf and sof pasuq are not counted. Ketiv/qere variants follow the qere reading; standalone s/pe paragraph markers are not counted.
2. **Value:** G is the sum of the Hebrew letter values. Only consonants count.
3. **Final forms take their standard values:** ך = 20, ם = 40, ן = 50, ף = 80, ץ = 90 — **not** 500–900.
   - Check: תַּחְלִיף ("substitute") = 400 + 8 + 30 + 10 + 80 = **528**, matching the source example. If ף were counted as 800, the word would be 1248, which does not match.
4. **Seat:** m14 = G mod 14 places the value on the fourteen-seat table. m7 = G mod 7 names the cluster. Because m7 is derived from m14, a "double X" cluster is a naming convention, **not** a second independent test.
5. **Checks:** prime/composite and factorization; mod 42 (3×14); mod 49 (7×7); joint checks +441 (truth) and +600 (deceiver).

> **Note on the repository's main README.** Its gematria table follows the earlier convention (final forms 500–900). This Ezekiel edition follows the 2026-08-20 correction. The two conventions differ only for final forms; the seat method is otherwise unchanged.

## Four rules

1. **Decoding is lossy.** Revelation may be without error; decoding is not. This edition is one family of checkable, lossy readings.
2. **Seat is not value.** A seat is a structural coordinate, not a moral verdict. Landing at 12 does not mean "false"; landing at 8 does not mean "resurrection."
3. **Same number is not same identity.** One value can point in opposite directions. The test is oath and fruit, not the number. Examples: גאל (redeem) and בבל (Babylon) are both 34; חטא (sin) and חי (live) are both 18.
4. **No identification of persons.** Gog, Magog, the prince of Tyre and the false prophets are seat structures, not labels for any living individual or group.

## Six results worth checking first

1. **The 575 formula.** Thirty-eight verses read exactly ויהי דבר־יהוה אלי לאמר, all G=575, m14=1, m7=1. The cluster name for a composite double-one is "self-certifying / usurping" — which would make every arrival of the word self-certification. The data itself therefore forces rule 2: seat is not value. The formula's last word לאמר = **271**, the value given to "the true Jeremiah."
2. **רוח = טהר = 214.** Spirit/wind and cleansing share 214, m14=4. Ezekiel 36:25-27 (clean water and new spirit), 37:1-14 (wind on the bones) and 39:12 (cleansing the land) are pinned to one gate seat.
3. **עצם = צדוק = קסם = יצק = 200.** Bones, Zadok, divination and pouring all stand at the gate. The gate is a contested entrance, not a reserved one.
4. **שער = רשע = עשר = רעש = 570.** The temple gate, the wicked, "ten" and the earthquake of 37:7 share seat 10.
5. **חיים = חלל = חכם = 68.** Life, the slain and wisdom share seat 12. Reading 12 as "false" would make life false — exactly the reading rule 2 blocks.
6. **יהוה שמה = 371.** The final name of the city, "The LORD is there," lands at 7 with m7=0 — the I AM signature. The book ends on the signature of truth, not on the dimensions of any city.

## What this edition does not claim

It cannot infer from a seat that the revelation is true. It cannot use a seat to identify any person. It cannot declare on God's behalf "who is." If a landing is read as "therefore the revelation is verified," rule 3 has been crossed; if a seat-12 landing is read as "therefore it is false," rule 2 has been crossed.

The end of decoding is not a verdict. It is to return the text to the text and judgment to God.
